Projects

OSGA

In our unit, indirect contacts (repeat prescriptions, test results, paperwork) used to get lost between professionals. With Ana Póvoa and Alice Avanzo I built OSGA, a shared queue where every request has an owner and gets reviewed regularly. We worked in Scrum and I was the Product Owner. OSGA is now part of daily work at USF Carcavelos, and we are preparing an evaluation of its impact.

We presented it to the ULS Lisboa Ocidental technical council and at a meeting with the SNS Executive Direction. It won best oral communication (practice report) at the 43rd APMGF National Meeting in 2026, and we took it to WONCA Europe 2026.

Teaching AI to clinicians

I'm a trainer on the mandatory digital health and AI course for second-year family medicine residents in Lisbon and Tagus Valley, and I coordinate the APMGF digital health study group.
