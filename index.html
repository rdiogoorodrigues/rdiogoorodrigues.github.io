Featured · Scrum Product Owner

OSGA: managing indirect contacts in family medicine

Indirect contacts (prescription renewals, test results, admin requests) overload family health units and get lost between professionals. With Ana Póvoa and Alice Avanzo I built OSGA using Scrum, as Product Owner: a visible queue with owners and a review cadence. It is implemented and in use at USF Carcavelos; an outcome evaluation is in preparation.
