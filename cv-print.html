Projects

OSGA

In our unit, indirect contacts (repeat prescriptions, test results, paperwork) used to get lost between professionals. With Ana Póvoa and Alice Avanzo I built OSGA, a shared queue where every request has an owner and gets reviewed regularly. We worked in Scrum and I was the Product Owner. It is now part of daily work at USF Carcavelos, and we are preparing an evaluation of its impact. Presented to the ULS Lisboa Ocidental technical council and the SNS Executive Direction, and at WONCA Europe 2026.
